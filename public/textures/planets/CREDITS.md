Files:

- `moonmap1k.jpg`, `moonbump1k.jpg` — Moon color + bump
- `marsmap1k.jpg`, `marsbump1k.jpg` — Mars color + bump
- `saturnmap.jpg`, `saturnringcolor.jpg`, `saturnringpattern.gif` — Saturn + rings
- `earthmap1k.jpg`, `earthbump1k.jpg`, `earthcloudmaptrans.jpg` — Earth
- `sunmap.jpg` — Sun

Used by `src/components/three/CelestialBody.tsx` for the score-driven 3D hero.
